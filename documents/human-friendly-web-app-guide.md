# ZakTec Solutions Human-Friendly Web App Guide

## Purpose of this guide

This guide explains the ZakTec Solutions website for clients, visitors and other non-technical stakeholders. It describes what the website is for, who it helps and how people use it.

Technical implementation details are kept in `technical-ai-document.md`. Brand presentation rules are kept in `branding.md`.

## What the website does

The website introduces ZakTec Solutions, a UK AI and digital consultancy for businesses and organisations.

It helps visitors:

- Understand the problems ZakTec Solutions can help with
- Compare the main services
- See examples of developing websites and platforms
- Learn how a project would be assessed and delivered
- Contact ZakTec Solutions about a project
- Express interest in collaborating with the company

The website is designed to explain services clearly before a visitor starts a conversation.

## Who the website is for

The website is written for:

- Small and medium-sized businesses
- Charities and community organisations
- Education and training providers
- Professional service businesses
- Public-sector organisations
- Independent specialists and possible delivery partners

Listing an audience does not mean that ZakTec Solutions already works with every type of organisation shown.

## What ZakTec Solutions offers

### AI Consultancy and Automation

ZakTec Solutions helps an organisation decide whether AI could improve a real task or process.

Examples include:

- Reducing repetitive administration
- Helping staff find approved information
- Supporting common enquiries
- Connecting suitable steps in a workflow
- Giving staff guidance on responsible AI use

AI is not recommended for every task. The need, risks and expected benefit are considered first.

### Digital Systems

Digital systems help an organisation collect information, organise work and deliver services.

Examples include:

- Dashboards
- Online forms
- Booking systems
- Client or member areas
- Reporting tools
- Progress tracking

For example, information that is currently copied between several spreadsheets may be easier to manage through one clear process.

### Websites and Online Services

ZakTec Solutions creates and improves websites that explain services clearly and help visitors take the right next step.

This can include:

- New business websites
- Charity or community websites
- Website redesigns
- Enquiry forms
- Clearer service pages
- Loading-speed improvements
- Basic search setup

### Training and Support

Training and support can be included where a project requires staff to understand a new tool, workflow or responsible use of AI.

## How a visitor uses the website

### Finding the right service

A visitor can start with the problem they want to solve.

For example:

- “Our staff repeat the same administrative task” may lead to AI Consultancy or Digital Systems.
- “Our information is spread across documents and spreadsheets” may lead to Digital Systems.
- “Customers cannot understand our services” may lead to Websites.
- “We are not sure what we need” can be selected on the Contact form.

Visitors do not need to choose a specific technology before making contact.

### Viewing projects

Project buttons on the homepage open the matching project details on the Projects page. Public external project links will only be added after their correct destinations are confirmed.

### Making an enquiry

The visitor:

1. Opens the Contact page.
2. Enters their name, reply email, organisation and project details.
3. Chooses the type of help they need.
4. Selects **Open Email Application**.
5. Reviews and sends the message from their own email application.

The website does not currently store the form details or show a confirmation page. Selecting the button does not send the message automatically.

## How projects are approached

The website describes four stages:

1. **Understand** — learn about the organisation, users, current systems and challenges.
2. **Assess and Plan** — identify suitable improvements and agree scope, priorities and next steps.
3. **Deliver** — build or configure the agreed work and test it with intended users.
4. **Support** — provide documentation, training and ongoing help where required.

The exact scope, cost and responsibilities must be agreed in writing before work begins.

## Benefits for visitors

The website gives visitors:

- A clear explanation of available services
- A problem-led way to find relevant help
- Honest status information for developing projects
- A simple overview of the project process
- A direct route to discuss requirements
- Clear information about responsible AI use

It avoids promises of guaranteed results because the right outcome depends on the organisation, its users, available information and project requirements.

## Current limitations

The website does not currently provide:

- User accounts
- A client dashboard
- Online payments
- Online booking
- Live chat
- An enquiry confirmation page
- Automatic storage of Contact form submissions

These should not be described as available unless they are added in the future.
